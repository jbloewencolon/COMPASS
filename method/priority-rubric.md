# Draft one-page priority rubric

**Method version:** `0.1.0-draft` · **Policy review status:** draft, agency review pending.
**Agency approval and decision owner:** not recorded. This is a manual discussion
aid, not an algorithm or an inherited Georgia score band.

For each finding, state the affected people/activity, worst credible consequence,
mechanism, evidence, time horizon and uncertainty. Keep impact separate from how
confidently a duty is established. Do not multiply severity and likelihood or
combine source authority, certainty and tractability into a numeric score.

| Dimension | Anchors and required rationale |
|---|---|
| Severity: critical | Credible loss of life, major safety harm, or lasting/irreversible loss of an essential right/service. Identify who bears it and why the scenario is credible. |
| Severity: high | Material denial/delay of service or rights, serious confidentiality exposure, or substantial operational harm with a supported recovery path. |
| Severity: moderate | Bounded harm or service disruption with a practical correction, while still identifying affected people and access barriers. |
| Severity: low | Limited, recoverable consequence; explain why a more serious outcome is not credibly supported. |
| Likelihood: observed/near | The mechanism is occurring, or a documented exposure and trigger make it credible within the stated review horizon. Cite the evidence rather than assert a percentage. |
| Likelihood: plausible | A supported mechanism and relevant exposure exist, but frequency/conditions remain uncertain. State what would change the assessment. |
| Likelihood: uncertain | Missing facts prevent a supported estimate. This is a request for information, not evidence that harm is rare or absent. |
| Concentration of harm | Optional: identify a small/disproportionately affected group and barriers to redress. A small population must not automatically lower priority. |
| Irreversibility | Optional: identify whether later correction can restore the lost interest, data, opportunity or safety. State the restoration limits. |

Assign a **proposed** response with a written rationale: immediate review/temporary
protection, next scheduled corrective work, or monitored follow-up with a concrete
trigger. Critical or irreversible consequences merit prompt review even when
likelihood is uncertain; uncertainty calls for investigation and a proportionate
interim measure, not automatic closure or a fabricated duty. Record an actual
deadline only when supported by a verified source or owner decision.

For tied items, consider first a supported near-term deadline or ongoing harm,
then concentrated/irreversible burden and dependencies that block other controls.
Use feasibility/effort to sequence comparable work, not to hide severe but costly
problems. Record why any tied item moves ahead and who decided; unchanged ties
can remain ties.

Record separately: source type/authority, verification, legal status and dates,
applicability/conditions, and evidence uncertainty. A persuasive source can support
a serious risk; strong authority does not make a minor consequence severe.
Missing review must remain pending. Agency policy review may revise these anchors
before adoption; this template neither accepts findings nor directs enforcement.
