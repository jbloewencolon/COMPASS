## Package sections

1. Decision memo: requested action, scope, options, dependencies, and resource needs.
2. Dashboard: one row per proposed amendment, its finding/source references,
   reviewed priority, owner, effort, and pending decision.
3. Amendment schedule and cards: current text, proposed text, exact source
   locations, rationale, conditions/exceptions, and consequential changes.
4. Contract or procurement annex where approved scope actually requires one.
5. Implementation plan: accountable roles, prerequisites, records, proposed dates,
   consultation, legal review, adoption, and publication steps.
6. Consequential edits, out-of-scope observations, and proposed register updates.

## Audience and resident-language review

Keep the executive explanation readable to a non-specialist policy owner. Keep
technical authority analysis precise, with definitions and full source locators.
For every notice, explanation, consent instruction or appeal/review route intended
for a resident, provide **the actual resident-facing text separately from the legal
provision**. If no resident-facing text is in scope, state that and explain why.

| Review field | Value to supply for each resident-facing passage |
|---|---|
| Audience and use | Intended reader, task, channel, relevant access/language needs and context |
| Draft text | Short sentences, active voice, clear actor/action and defined terms; do not replace legal precision with an inaccurate simplification |
| Reading-level note | Approximate target around grade 8 unless the agency supplies another target; record how estimated, limitations and any exceptions. An estimate is not a certified comprehension result. |
| Meaning-preservation check | Confirm scope, exceptions, dates, rights, contact/alternative route and governing definitions against verified text; never invent a remedy or deadline |
| Audience/accessibility notes | Undefined vocabulary, translation review where needed, non-digital alternatives, screen-reader/keyboard and format needs; identify user testing actually performed or still pending |
| Review status | Actual reviewer/date and unresolved questions, or explicitly pending; a blank is not a pass |

## Adversarial-reader pass

After drafting, examine each supported provision from the perspective of an
affected person challenging a decision, a records requester, an implementer, and
a supplier seeking the narrowest available reading. These are review perspectives,
not claims that counsel or affected residents have participated.

Record the passage, ambiguity or unmet condition, plausible alternative reading,
whether the promised control can be evidenced, and proposed correction. Test who
acts, when, against what standard, what record exists, and what supported consequence
or review route follows. Preserve counterarguments and unresolved issues. Avoid
promising a result the agency cannot demonstrate or a third-party duty with no
contract/procurement/legal hook.

Adversarial-review outcome: **not performed / pending** until an actual review is
recorded. A passage surviving this pass still requires the agency's authorized
policy/legal/adoption process; it does not gain approval from a template.
