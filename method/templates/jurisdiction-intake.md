# Jurisdiction intake worksheet

**Status:** draft; complete before using the method for a real jurisdiction.
**Method version:** `0.1.0-draft` — agency review pending; no approval recorded.
This is a manual worksheet, not a runtime configuration schema loaded by COMPASS.

| Item | Jurisdiction-specific information to supply |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction and office | Identifier, name, issuing/maintaining office, policy owner |
| Covered entities and activities | Agencies/programs, vendors, exclusions, shared-service interfaces |
| Instrument inventory | Stable IDs, titles, versions, source locations, dates, full text |
| Authority and hierarchy | Actual local instrument families; which may create duties and within what scope |
| Definitions | Exact defined terms, local terminology, non-equivalent external terms |
| Applicable sources | Statutes, regulations, orders, grant/contract conditions, adopted standards; known exceptions |
| Verification | Source type, verification status/date/checker, primary-source location, unresolved questions |
| Records and disclosure | Retention schedules, access restrictions, required artifacts, publication route |
| Legislative calendar | Annual or biennial sessions; actual session/biennium dates and typical adjournment; enactment/effective-date conventions, carryover/dead-bill rules, emergency/special sessions and verified official sources; unknown if not supplied |
| Review calendar | Owner-set cadence, off-cycle triggers and responsibility for checking the actual legislative calendar; do not copy Georgia dates |
| Tribal consultation and sovereignty | Whether any in-scope holding/activity requires consultation or consent, relevant tribes and agency-appointed liaison, actual authority/agreement and unresolved questions; no obligation inferred merely from a tribal reference |
| NAGPRA and cultural-resource material | Relevant holdings/activities and covered entity; NAGPRA, 25 U.S.C. § 3001 et seq., is an **unverified research lead**, not a confirmed duty for every agency. Supply applicable current authority, consultation/return processes, restricted site/human-remains information, and who verifies scope |
| Confidentiality and agreements | Source-specific access, disclosure, reuse and derivative-data restrictions; consultation, data-sharing, stewardship or custody agreements and their actual parties/versions; do not assume a universal exemption |
| Funding and program conditions | Funding agency, recipient/program, agreement/award and version, covered action/data, conditions and current verification; funding alone does not establish identical duties across programs |
| COMPASS's own records | Classification/retention of uploads, extracted text, prompts, model inputs/outputs if used, profiles/catalogues, reports, audit/decision/verification records and exports; schedule authority, hold/appeal obligations, access and disposal owner |
| Decision rights | Authorized decision-maker, review/delegation rules, counsel and procurement routes |
| Prior work | Baseline/run IDs, source and governance registers, unresolved/settled findings |
| Priority rubric | Reviewed version of the [draft rubric](../priority-rubric.md), severity/likelihood rationale, concentrated/irreversible harm, tie handling and separate authority/verification/applicability uncertainty |
| Coverage limitations | Missing sources, uncertain extraction, unknown applicability, unavailable expertise |

Policy-owner confirmation: **not recorded**.

Agency-appointed review lead, legal reviewer and tribal/program/records contacts:
**not supplied**. Record actual appointments and authority rather than inventing
a central counsel or treating a method template as delegation.

Do not copy dates, source applicability, or approval status from another
jurisdiction. Record each confirmation with a person, date, and supporting basis.
For each scope row, provide a primary locator/agreement reference or mark unknown
with the person/action needed to resolve it. An unsupported “not applicable” answer
must not remove a topic from the declared coverage sweep.
