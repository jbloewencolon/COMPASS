# 04: Amendment preparation

This is a human-operated drafting workflow template. It does not enable amendment
generation in the current application. Use the
[package outline](../templates/amendment-package.md).

## Inputs

Finding IDs and their actual decision records; complete current text of each
affected instrument; source/anchor and governance registers; jurisdiction
intake; local drafting, legal-review, procurement, and adoption requirements.

## Review instructions

1. Check who accepted each finding, when, and on what scope. If acceptance is not
   recorded, keep the finding OPEN and label any proposal **DRAFT FOR DECISION**.
   Do not manufacture decisions or implement an assumed approval.
2. For accepted scope, prepare proposed text in the appropriate local instrument.
   Use its actual authority, numbering, definitions, and obligation vocabulary.
3. Specify the accountable actor, trigger/timing, action/standard, retained record,
   and applicable consequence or existing cross-reference. Confirm feasibility.
4. Resolve references and definitions; show consequential edits and dependencies.
   Do not import another state's legal obligations or dated compliance examples.
5. Separate source type, verification status, and applicability. Verify each
   load-bearing citation and operative date before routing for legal review.

## Outputs

A draft decision memo, dashboard, proposed text/redlines, implementation needs,
and register updates. Leave decision fields uncompleted until the responsible
person acts. Record adoption and implementation as subsequent, distinct events.
