# 06: Horizon scan and source-register maintenance

**Method version:** `0.1.0-draft`
**Policy review status:** draft — agency review pending; no approval recorded.

**Status: draft neutral method; agency review required before operational use.**
This document adapts the research and source-maintenance stage of the preserved
[Georgia horizon scan](../../case-studies/georgia/research/Anchor-horizon-scan-v1.0.md).
It is a new template, not a corrected historical original or an executable feature.
Use [jurisdiction intake](../templates/jurisdiction-intake.md) and the relevant
source-specific [Georgia errata](../../case-studies/georgia/ERRATA.md) if relying on
that case. Historical “Prompt 03” remains unchanged; neutral Prompt 03 is trigger
assessment, so this adaptation is numbered 06.

## Purpose and authority

Find evidence that answers an identified policy question, resolves a source check,
changes a scoped obligation, or supplies clearly labeled optional practice. This
is a targeted research and source-maintenance exercise, not an instruction to
collect news, draft amendments, approve findings or modify an instrument.

The agency appoints the responsible review lead and any legal reviewer. Record
their actual names only when supplied. Do not invent a central counsel or approval.
All register changes are proposals until the person authorized by that agency
records the disposition. Preserve the source register version read and every
historical snapshot; do not overwrite old assertions with a new verification date.

## Inputs

- Intake, agency/program/action scope, review date, run ID, operator and appointed
  review roles, with missing appointments explicitly unresolved.
- Current source/anchor register, its actual version, prior scan and latest relevant
  baseline; no claim to have read a missing artifact.
- Current internal instruments, definitions and exceptions where the research
  question depends on precise text.
- Owner-approved standing briefs, unresolved source checks, watch items, hard-date
  queue and the agency's research priorities.
- Existing source errata keyed to exact artifact basename and hash. A preserved
  file hash proves identity, not current legal correctness.

## Scope and standing briefs

Use U.S. federal, state and District of Columbia sources as the primary legal
scope. Search official statutes/regulations, orders, court texts, agency materials
and source-specific current status. Standards, research and practice can inform
optional controls but do not become binding through a score or publication label.

Retain EU/other foreign material with a visible **non-U.S. comparative/vendor
context** label and its jurisdiction. Do not assume it binds the agency, assume it
can never affect a supplier, or discard original foreign references. Refer any
actual foreign reach claim for a separately supported applicability review.

For each standing brief, record a specific consumer: an instrument/provision,
pending finding, unresolved check or operational question. An item that is merely
“relevant to AI” has not established a reason for admission.

For a **contestability** brief, start with the existing U.S. authority applicable
to the exact agency action. Identify any protected interest, program/action scope,
notice/hearing/review trigger, timing, reviewer and remedy. Consider constitutional
procedural due process, the applicable state administrative procedure law, and
program rules such as Medicaid 42 C.F.R. part 431 subpart E or SNAP 7 C.F.R. §273.15
only where they cover the case. A federal-funding fact alone does not establish
identical hearing rights across programs. Federal-agency AI memoranda such as
OMB M-25-21 are persuasive state-policy comparators unless a separately verified
basis creates a state obligation; do not confuse a comparator with governing law.

## Research sequence

1. **Standing briefs.** Report an answer, partial progress, unresolved verification,
   or no new material for every brief. Preserve evidence and alternative readings.
2. **Unresolved checks.** Locate exact primary texts and current versions. Record
   whether a check was resolved and which claims depend on it. Retrieval failure
   does not establish invalidity or successful verification.
3. **Dates and legal stages.** Verify adoption, publication, effective/compliance
   dates, scope, stays, repeal and supersession separately. A date reaching the
   calendar does not itself establish that a conditional duty applies.
4. **Staleness.** Revisit source-specific stale records. Record a real check and its
   outcome, including no change, only when actually performed. Do not freshen a
   verification timestamp because an older source was copied.
5. **Targeted expansion.** Use remaining agreed capacity for research tied to a
   named consumer. Rank governing scoped evidence separately from optional practice.

Use primary locators for load-bearing claims. Secondary trackers may help find a
source; record their discovery role instead of disguising them as primary text.
For court/agency developments, preserve the exact text/status and refer its effect
to the agency-appointed reviewer rather than automatically declaring preemption,
invalidity or a new duty. No automated court/AG classifier is added by this template.

## Admission and verification record

Keep these dimensions separate for every candidate:

- Source type, issuer and jurisdiction; original title/identifier and version.
- Source URL/location, exact relevant passage, publication and operative dates.
- Verification state, actual checker/date when checked, and any retrieval limitation.
- Legal status and scoped applicability, including conditions, exceptions,
  uncertainty and a reference to any agency review.
- The specific provision/finding/brief/check served and why the evidence matters.
- Proposed treatment: add, watch, amend, retire, retain unchanged or reject.

No evidence tier, final-publication label, citation match or numerical priority can
create binding force. Keep speculative material in a watch list with an observable
trigger; do not promote it to an obligation. An unverified figure remains unverified
and must not be repeated as established evidence.

Agree per-run capacity through intake. The historical 12-addition/6-watch ceilings
may be considered as starting constraints, but they are not default law or proof
that the register is complete. If capacity limits the work, list and prioritize the
unreviewed material rather than implying it was checked or found irrelevant.

## Outputs

Produce a dated research report with these clearly separated blocks:

1. **Run metadata and limits:** run ID, operator, agency, scope, inputs/versions,
   baseline reference, source checks actually completed, missing inputs and capacity.
2. **ADD-PROPOSED:** candidate source, primary locator, version, separate status/
   verification/applicability fields, intended consumer and reason for admission.
3. **WATCH-PROPOSED:** item, jurisdiction, current uncertainty, observable trigger,
   expected date if known and proposed owner; do not invent an appointment.
4. **CHECK outcomes:** original question, verified resolution or unresolved reason,
   actual source/checker/date where available, and dependent claims to revisit.
5. **AMEND/RETIRE-PROPOSED:** existing source key, exact text at issue, proposed
   correction, evidence, affected findings and an explicitly pending disposition.
6. **Standing-brief and reject log:** question, sources searched, result, carry-forward
   status; rejected item, reason and condition for reconsideration.

Continue existing IDs without reuse and preserve original source keys. Do not
open, close, approve or rescore governance findings as a side effect of a scan.
Refer potential finding changes to the appropriate assessment and record who later
accepts a register change. Report U.S. governing candidates and non-U.S. context
separately without deleting historical evidence.

## Completion checks

Confirm that each proposed addition has a consumer; no unsupported duty or universal
benefit/funding rule was introduced; dates and scope remain distinct; foreign
context is labeled; unverified sources remain unverified; and all dispositions
await actual authorized action. State what could not be checked and carry the
question forward. A completed research report is not approval of its proposals.
