# 05: Legislative session readiness

Schedule this review using the jurisdiction's actual legislative calendar,
prefiling rules, carryover rules, and policy owner's planning window.

## Inputs

Most recent baseline and both registers; current instruments; jurisdiction intake;
official legislative records; known inventory, staffing, procurement, and delivery
constraints. Identify any missing baseline before claiming readiness.

## Review instructions

1. Verify the status and scope of relevant proposals. Distinguish introduced,
   carried over, failed, enacted, and future-effective measures under the local
   rules. Do not treat anticipated legislation as an existing obligation.
2. Map plausible requirements to concrete artifacts: inventories, usage plans,
   reporting processes, model policies, oversight records, or other specified
   deliverables. Preserve each proposal's actual scope and uncertainty.
3. Compare readiness with the baseline and current evidence. Identify missing
   inputs, owners, dependencies, estimated effort, and resource assumptions.
4. Separate useful preparation from actions that require a future enactment or
   an authorized policy decision. State the trigger for reconsideration.

## Outputs

A sourced readiness matrix and proposed preparation plan. Link each item to
the proposal, current artifact, readiness gap, proposed owner/date, and approval
dependency. Keep prospective scenarios separate from current legal duties.
