# 02: Scheduled surveillance

**Method version:** `0.1.0-draft`
**Policy review status:** draft — agency review pending; no approval recorded.

Use the cadence set in the [jurisdiction intake](../templates/jurisdiction-intake.md).
No month or legislative-session variant is assumed by this template.

## Inputs

Review interval and run ID; most recent baseline; current source/anchor and
governance registers; watch-list triggers; relevant instrument text where a
change turns on exact wording.

If the baseline is missing, record that dependency as missing. Do not present a
new comparison as a verified delta from a baseline that was not inspected.

## Review instructions

1. Check the watch list and approved sources. Record what changed and what was
   checked with no material change, including dates and source locations.
2. Distinguish text changes, legal-stage/date changes, source corrections, and
   changes in scope or conditions. Evaluate applicability for this jurisdiction.
3. Link each material delta to the affected provision, finding, deferral, or
   dependency. Include supersession, stays, exceptions, and uncertainty.
4. Recommend no action, continued monitoring with a concrete trigger, assigned
   follow-up with a proposed date, or escalation. Do not manufacture changes.

## Outputs

A concise delta brief, source/register corrections, proposed follow-ups, and
the next watch list. Preserve source versions and baseline references. A proposed
disposition is not a recorded policy-owner decision.
