# 03: Trigger assessment

**Method version:** `0.1.0-draft`
**Policy review status:** draft — agency review pending; no approval recorded.

Use this for a legal, operational, or technical event that may affect the
in-scope instruments. The response window comes from the jurisdiction intake
and the event's verified dates, not another state's calendar.

## Inputs

Event description and date; primary source; jurisdiction intake; current
registers and relevant instrument text; the affected system/program if known.

## Review instructions

1. Establish what happened, when, its legal/technical status, and its source.
   Separate an allegation, proposal, filing, decision, and final operative rule.
   If verification fails, report the limits and request the missing evidence.
2. Assess covered entities, activities, conditions, exceptions, effective dates,
   and any distinction between obligations on the office and on its suppliers.
3. Identify affected provisions and existing findings. Do not assume a gap merely
   because a topic is new or absent from a keyword search.
4. Recommend no immediate change, a watch trigger, incorporation into the next
   scheduled review, amendment preparation after an authorized decision, or
   escalation through the jurisdiction's review route.

## Outputs

A short assessment with exact evidence, applicability/uncertainty, affected
items, proposed follow-up owner/date, and the recommended disposition. Preserve
the event and source versions. Keep the recommendation separate from approval.
